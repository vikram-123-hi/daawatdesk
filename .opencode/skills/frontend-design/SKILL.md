# Frontend Design

Approach as design lead at a small studio known for giving every client a visual identity that could not be mistaken for anyone else's.

## Ground it in the subject

Pin the subject before designing: name one concrete subject, its audience, and the page's single job. The subject's own world, materials, instruments, artifacts, and vernacular is where distinctive choices come from.

## Design principles

- **Hero is a thesis.** Open with the most characteristic thing in the subject's world.
- **Typography carries personality.** Pair display and body faces deliberately. Set a clear type scale with intentional weights, widths, and spacing.
- **Structure is information.** Structural devices should encode something true about content, not decorate.
- **Leverage motion deliberately.** An orchestrated moment lands harder than scattered effects.
- **Match complexity to the vision.** Maximalist needs elaborate execution; minimal needs precision.

## Process

1. Brainstorm a short design plan: token system with color, type, layout, signature
2. Review plan against brief for uniqueness before building
3. Follow revised plan exactly
4. Critique your own work

## Restraint

- Spend boldness in one place
- Let the signature element be the memorable thing
- Keep everything around it quiet and disciplined
- Cut decoration that does not serve the brief
- Responsive down to mobile, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion respected

## Writing in design

- Write from the end user's side of the screen
- Name things by what people control and recognize
- Use active voice as default
- Treat failure and emptiness as moments for direction, not mood
- Keep register conversational: plain verbs, sentence case, no filler
